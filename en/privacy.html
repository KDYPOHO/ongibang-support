Privacy Policy

Applies to Ongibang 1.3 · Effective when version 1.3 is released. This policy also describes the 1.3 TestFlight build.

Version 1.3 was submitted for iOS and Mac review on October 10, 2026 and is awaiting review. Version 1.1 currently uses independent local storage without automatic sync. Its Korean privacy policy remains available.

1. Records on your device

Ongibang stores memory text, warmth selections, photos, plans and completion states, optional moods and day reflections on your device. It has no separate account, advertising, tracking or usage analytics.

2. Optional private iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud, records and photos are sent to Apple's private CloudKit database and synchronized between your devices on the same Apple Account. The developer cannot view other users' private records. Apple services and account settings apply.

Offline edits save locally first. Turning off sync keeps local and existing iCloud records. Changing Apple Accounts pauses sync; records are not automatically sent to the new account.

3. Photos

Only images you choose through the system picker or file picker are copied into the app. Saved JPEG copies are independent of the original photo.

4. Editing and deletion

Memories, plans and reflections can be edited or deleted in the app. Deletion history syncs when iCloud is enabled so stale devices do not restore deleted records. Deleting the app does not delete previously saved iCloud data. iCloud storage can also be managed through Apple's account and device settings.

5. Backup files

Backups are unencrypted JSON files containing photos and records, saved to a location you choose. Exported files are managed separately and are not deleted when an app record is deleted. Version 1.1 backups are supported; 1.0 backups are not automatically converted. OS device backups may include app data according to system settings.

6. Notifications and synchronization state

Plan reminders are scheduled locally on devices where permission is granted. Device-specific reminder preferences, sync queue state and the Apple Account's CloudKit record identifier are stored locally to manage syncing and pause it on account changes. Internal random device identifiers are used only to resolve edits in your private database, not for tracking.

7. Support and this website

Email todaklife@gmail.com. Contact details and messages you send are used to answer your request. You don't need to send personal records or photos. Request deletion of your support message at the same address.

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages, with no advertising, analytics or personal input forms. The hosting provider's privacy policy applies to its service operations. Demo memories are examples; the rest session is kept only during your visit.

8. Changes

Updates to this policy identify the applicable version and effective date. Operator: 강동엽. Contact: todaklife@gmail.com.
