Start where you are.

On first use, choose Use iCloud or Keep on this device. You can change this in Settings later. Today holds your plans and reflections; Room is your place to rest; Library holds all memories; Settings holds sync, reminders and backup.

You don't need an Ongibang account or a daily record. iPhone, iPad and Mac require OS version 27 or later.

A small plan for today.

Use Add a plan for a title and date, optional time, notes, repeat and reminder. Repeat choices are Never, Every day, Weekdays and Weekly on the same day. A quick title field adds a plan for the selected date.

Mark complete and Mark incomplete apply to that date's occurrence. Repeating plans have independent completion states. Editing affects unfinished and future occurrences; completed titles remain as they were. Deleting a recurring plan stops future repeats and keeps completed history.

Use the date selector, search or Planned and Completed filters. Older unfinished plans keep their original dates; they are not moved automatically. Show older plans loads more repeating occurrences.

Reminders need notification permission and a time. Receive plan reminders is a separate preference on each device. Upcoming repeats are scheduled first, up to 60 pending reminders, and replenished on launch, foreground and changes. Open the app periodically to replenish them.

For the you who lived this day.

Finish my day lets you look back at completed plans, optionally choose a mood and write a sentence. Leave the fields blank if you like. Rest without writing goes straight to a quiet minute.

View day reflections to edit or delete past reflections. Keep as a memory copies your sentence to Library as Archive only. Deleting a plan does not delete your reflection.

A moment to return to.

Use Add a memory. A sentence or one photo is enough. Choose a photo or Choose a photo file imports your selected image; Remove photo removes it from the edited memory.

Photos are copied into the app as JPEGs up to 2048 pixels on the long edge. Original files may be up to 50 MB. Deleting the original does not remove the saved copy.

Archive only is the default. Only memories assigned to rest, encouragement or connection appear by the blanket, lamp or mug. Library supports search, filtering, editing and deletion.

Your records, in one file.

  1. Open Settings and choose Export backup.
  2. Save the JSON file to a safe location.
  3. On another device, choose Import backup and select that file.

Version 1.3 exports backup format 2, with memories, photos, plans, completed occurrences and reflections. Format 1 from version 1.1 is also accepted. Version 1.0 backups are not automatically converted. The maximum file size is 200 MB.

Files are validated before import. Existing IDs and deleted record IDs are not overwritten. Backups are not encrypted. Deleting records in the app does not delete previously exported files.

Your devices, together.

Enable Sync across my devices in Settings on each device using the same Apple Account. Records are stored in Apple's private CloudKit database. Offline edits save locally first and upload when a connection is available. Sync can take time; it isn't an instant transfer.

Settings shows status, pending records, last successful sync and Retry sync. Notification preferences remain separate on each device. Turning sync off leaves local and existing iCloud records intact.

If your Apple Account changes, sync pauses. Local records are not automatically transferred to the new account. Send local records to the new Apple Account is an explicit choice.

Version 1.1 stores each device independently. For 1.1, use its backup export and import controls to move memories.

Take a quiet minute.

Choose Just rest in Room or Take a little rest with Oni in Today. The session is sixty seconds without sound. Return to your room or Escape on Mac ends it at any time. Completion stays visible until you leave.

App inactivity pauses the rest clock and movement. Reduce Motion uses static expressions and emphasis. The website's Pause motion controls only animation; leaving the tab pauses its rest timer.

When something needs a little care.

A memory isn't in my comfort flow.

Only memories assigned to that warmth appear there. Archive only memories remain in Library.

My other device hasn't updated.

Check the Apple Account, iCloud settings, network and available iCloud storage. Open Ongibang on both devices and use Retry sync. Local records remain available while offline. Reconnection can be delayed; if pending records remain, use Retry sync after the network returns. If an initial receive error appears, retry and contact support if it persists.

A reminder didn't arrive.

Check Receive plan reminders, notification permission, the plan's time and reminder switch. Focus and system notification settings can silence notifications. Reopen the app to replenish pending repeats.

I can't import a backup.

Use an original format 1 or 2 Ongibang JSON file under 200 MB. Damaged or unsupported files are rejected without partially importing them. Export again from the original device.
